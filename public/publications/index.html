Openings

Ph.D. Positions: One fully funded Ph.D. position is available in COSINE Lab starting from Spring 2026 or Fall 2026. If you’re passionate about complex systems, network science, or data-driven design, we’d love to hear from you! To apply, please email me at Yinshuang.Xiao@colostate.edu with the subject line “GRA Application to Cosine Lab.” and include your CV, transcripts, and a brief summary of your research interests and experience. For detailed requirements, please refer to the Call_For_Application. We’re excited to welcome new Cosiners to our team!

Undergrad and MS students: If COSINE research topics excite you, don’t hesitate to reach out at Yinshuang.Xiao@colostate.edu with the subject line “Perspective Undergrad / MS to Cosine Lab.” I’m always happy to chat about research opportunities, collaborations, or just share more about our work.
